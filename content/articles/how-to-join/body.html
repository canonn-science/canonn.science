How to Join

This page previously described a multi-step website registration process for joining Canonn. That process is now obsolete. Joining Canonn is done entirely through Discord.

Join us on Discord

You do not have to be a member of Canonn to visit our Discord server and see all the work we are doing. Visitors can view everything going on in the laboratories and can talk in a few public channels.

If you want to join in with the research in the laboratories, you will need to become a full member. Membership is quick and free, and it all happens on Discord:

  1. Join the Canonn Discord server.
  2. Read the #server_rules channel and the Welcome pack.
  3. Post a message in the #newcomers_welcome channel with the colour you would like for your username. A Canonn moderator will then give you the right roles and you will have access to the full server.

Note that by joining our Discord or any of our player groups you implicitly agree to our Guidelines for Website and Player Group Usage. Please also take a look at our optional donation information.

If you have any problems, please reach out to us via Discord.
