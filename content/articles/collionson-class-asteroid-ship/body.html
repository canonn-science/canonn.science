The Collionson Class Asteroid Ship is a type of Asteroid Miner.

  • Note: Your ship’s navigation system may display the Collionson Class Asteroid Ship with two different spellings, "Collionson" and "Collinson", depending on where the megaship is targeted. The "Collionson" spelling will only appear when targeting the megaship via the navigation panel (unless the megaship has been renamed, in which case the "Collionson" spelling will not be displayed at all), however when targeting via the contacts panel - the spelling will instead be "Collinson".

The class consists of a command module on the front, with a asteroid behind it which has two defence turrets, two cargo bays and two bio domes, this is followed by a storage module, a habitation ring with 3 bio domes, a second storage module, and finally by a drive module with another defence turret and cargo bay.

Currently, only two Collinson Class Asteroid Ship's have been reported, the first was reported to be within the Gateway system, orbiting body Hope (1), between 28-Feb-3303 and 26-Sep-3303 (estimated). After which time the megaship disappeared, believed to have travelled onwards, destination unknown.

  • Note: This megaship was actually reported as being present within the Gateway system during the 2.3 Beta (28-Feb-3303), with the earliest known video evidence being dated 04-Mar-3303 (2017). However, as this was during a Beta period and not on the Live servers, due to that fact, this information is only recorded here for record keeping.

The second Collinson Class Asteroid Ship, named "Perpetual Toil", was discovered within the permit locked LFT 509 system, orbiting body B 2 a, on 29-Oct-3312. The permit for this system was given out by the Pilots' Federation on 29-Oct-3312, at the request of investigating the system.

The following information is based on the Perpetual Toil, as it is the only known Collinson Class Asteroid Ship that is accessible at this time (Nov 3312).

  • Number of Turrets: 3
  • Cargo Bays: 3
  • Hackable Data Transmitters: 2
  • Ship Log Uplinks: 6
  • Default Cargo:
    • Metals (Beryllium, Copper, Gallium, Lithium, Palladium, Silver, Tantalum, Titanium),
    • Minerals (Bauxite, Bromellite, Cryolite, Goslarite, Indite, Lepidolite, Pyrophyllite, Uraninite)
  • Rare Commodities:
    • Chemicals (Delta Phoenicis Palms, HIP Organophosphates, Toxandji Virocide),
    • Machinery (Giant Verrix, Non Euclidian Exotanks, Volkhab Bee Drones, Wulpa Hyperbore Systems),
    • Technology (AZ Cancri Formula 42)
