Dove Enigma is a dockable megaship in Colonia around 3 a, added to Colonia as part of the Enigma Expedition.

The megaship is named in memory of CMDR DoveEnigma13, a Canonn member who passed away in 2018. As well as the Enigma Expedition, he is remembered for the memorial megaship bearing his name. Dove Enigma was UA bombed, and many commanders worked to bring it back online in time for his arrival at the end of the expedition, where an honour guard was formed to meet him.
